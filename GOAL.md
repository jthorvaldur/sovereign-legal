# GOAL

Build a working jurisdictional analysis toolkit:

1. **Identify** which jurisdiction a court operates in (Natural, Common, Statutory, Commercial).
2. **Translate** legal terms across all four jurisdictional layers — same word, different meaning, different rights.
3. **Generate questions** that challenge jurisdiction and force the court to disclose its authority.
4. **Document the 4-layer model** as a referenceable framework for all legal analysis across the ecosystem.

The toolkit should be CLI-first, composable with other repos (words_quantum_legal, div_legal, caseledger), and backed by vector search across 2M+ indexed legal documents.
